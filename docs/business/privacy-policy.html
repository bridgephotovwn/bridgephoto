BRIDGE PHOTO Business – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026. Applies to BRIDGE PHOTO Business for Android (package app.bridgephoto.business), version 1.2 and later, until this page is replaced.

The short version

  • BRIDGE PHOTO Business collects no personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server.
  • The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send or receive data.
  • It uses the camera only in its own capture tools, only while you have one of them open, and the pictures stay on your phone.
  • Your scans stay in the app's private storage on your phone. They leave the phone only when you share or export them.
  • Scanning, text recognition and page finding run on your phone.

1. Who publishes BRIDGE PHOTO Business

BRIDGE PHOTO Business is published by the developer shown on Google Play as BRIDGE PHOTO, an individual developer with a personal Google Play developer account. It is a separate app from BRIDGE PHOTO (app.bridgephoto), which has its own privacy policy. BRIDGE PHOTO Business is currently free to install and has no ads and no in-app purchases.

Questions about this policy: bridgephoto.vwn@gmail.com.

2. What data the app collects

None. The app has no sign-in, no user account and no server. It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, device identifiers or usage data, and it contains no analytics or advertising code.

The app does not use the internet. Its manifest does not keep the INTERNET permission (a Google library adds it, and the app's build removes it again), so the operating system does not let it open a network connection. Nothing you scan, type or recognise is sent to us or to anyone else by the app.

If the app ever closes unexpectedly, it keeps a short technical report (the time, what went wrong in the code, the app version, the Android version and the phone model) in its private storage and shows it the next time you open the app. The report goes nowhere unless you choose Share, and then only where you send it.

We, the developer, learn nothing about you from your use of the app. The only things we can see are what Google Play shows every developer: aggregate install statistics, public reviews you choose to write, and crash reports from people who have chosen in their Android settings to share diagnostics with Google. That information comes from Google and is covered by Google's privacy policy.

3. Permissions

  • Camera. Android asks for it the first time you open one of the app's own capture tools: the guided scan (the phone says where to move it, for people who cannot see the screen), Read a seal or stamped plate and Take the glare off. The camera is used only while one of those screens is open. The pictures are processed on your phone and kept only as the pages you take. If you refuse, those tools do not work; everything else does. The ordinary scanning screen is provided by Google Play services (section 5) and needs no permission from the app.
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE), added by a Google library: it only lets an app ask whether the phone is online and cannot move data.
  • Internet: not kept. The app cannot make network calls.
  • Photos and files: importing a photo or a PDF goes through the scanner's gallery option or Android's file picker; exporting and sharing go through Android's save dialog or share sheet. These need no app permission, and the app only ever sees the files you pick.

The app never asks for your location, contacts, microphone or anything else.

4. Where your scans are stored

Scans, page images, recognised text and the files the app builds (PDF, Word, Excel) are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read that folder. The app keeps no cloud copy.

Android backup is switched off for this app, so your scans are not copied to Google's backup service. This also means that uninstalling the app deletes all of its scans. Export or share anything you want to keep before you uninstall. Deleting a document or a page inside the app moves it to Recently deleted, from where it can be restored or removed for good.

5. On-device processing

BRIDGE PHOTO Business uses these components, all of which run on your phone:

  • Google ML Kit Document Scanner (part of Google Play services) provides the ordinary scanning screen: automatic capture, edge detection, corner adjustment, filters, multi-page capture and import from the gallery.
  • Google ML Kit Text Recognition (part of Google Play services) reads the text on your pages (Latin, Devanagari, Chinese, Japanese and Korean scripts) so that files can be searched and text can be copied.
  • Tesseract, an open-source recogniser built into the app with its language files, reads Arabic pages and helps read Nepali ones. It is ordinary code inside the app, downloads nothing and reports nothing.
  • The app's own image code finds the page in the camera picture, flattens folds and curves, and builds your PDF, Word and Excel files.

The first time they are needed, Google Play services downloads the scanner and text-recognition models to your phone, once. That download is made by Google Play services, not by this app. Google publishes what its ML Kit components report to Google: its ML Kit Android data disclosure lists device information, the app's package name and version, per-installation identifiers and technical details such as image sizes, timings and error codes, for diagnostics and usage analytics. Your images and your recognised text are not in that list. We never receive any of it. It is Google's software, under the Google Privacy Policy.

Spoken directions. The guided scan speaks short, fixed instructions such as "Move left" or "Hold still". With a screen reader on (such as TalkBack), they are spoken by your screen reader; with none on, by the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone. Only those instructions are spoken, never the content of your documents. The speech engine is a separate app on your phone, under its maker's policy.

Because of this design the app needs Google Play services. It does not work on Android phones that do not have it.

6. Sharing and exporting

Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to share, export or save it. When you share a scan or its text to WhatsApp, email or another app, that app receives the file and handles it under its own privacy policy. When you export to a folder, the file is written where you chose. We do not see, receive or log what you share, export or save.

7. Children

BRIDGE PHOTO Business is a general-purpose tool for work documents. It is not designed for or marketed to children. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

8. Security

Your scans are protected by your phone: its screen lock and device encryption. PDFs you export can also be given a password in the app. The app has no server and keeps no copy of your data, so there is no server for anyone to break into.

9. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. You are in full control on your phone: delete scans inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored. For anything Google Play or Google Play services collects, use Google's tools and the policies linked above.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if a future version needs the internet or sells features inside the app, we will update this page first, change the effective date at the top, and describe the change in the app's release notes. The current version of this policy is always the one published at this address.
